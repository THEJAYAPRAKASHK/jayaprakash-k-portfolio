About

A closer look

Biography, outlook, and the principles that guide how I work and grow.

01 Biography

I hold a Master of Business Administration (MBA) and a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA), giving me a well-rounded foundation across management principles, strategy, operations and organisational behaviour. My academic journey has been driven by genuine curiosity about how organisations make decisions and how people work together to achieve them.

02 Career Journey

My path began with the fundamentals of business administration at the undergraduate level, where I built a working vocabulary for management, marketing and finance. That foundation led me into postgraduate study, where I learned to think more strategically — connecting theory with real decisions through case studies and applied projects.

03 Vision & Mission

Vision: To build a career defined by sound judgement, steady growth and genuine contribution — the kind of professional others can rely on.

Mission: To keep learning deliberately, to apply what I learn with integrity, and to treat every stage of my career as a foundation for the next.
